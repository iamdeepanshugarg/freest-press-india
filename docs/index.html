Reliability vs independence guide

This is a reading aid, not a political bias ranking. Use evidence and caveats before trusting any source.

High transparency + strong independence
Prioritise, but still verify article-level claims.
High transparency + corporate/legacy
Useful with ownership and paywall caveats.
Low transparency + independent claim
Needs ownership/correction review.
Government/platform tools
Useful for official or technical checks, not neutral truth by default.
